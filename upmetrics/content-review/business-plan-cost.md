## WHAT'S WORKING

- **Neutrality is genuinely protected.** Upmetrics is named once, without adjectives, and the article never implies the software route is the right answer. This is the whole strategic bet of the page and the writer honoured it.
- **The "you don't have to use one route for the entire plan" insight is original.** No competitor in the SERP unbundles the plan this way, and only a company not selling plan-writing hours would offer it.
- **The consulting ceiling is fixed.** Dropping from the live page's $25,000-$50,000 to $5,000-$25,000+ removes a real credibility liability and now sits inside verifiable market range.
- **The funding-guarantee FAQ refuses the vendor framing.** A flat "No" plus a warning about anyone promising approval is exactly right on a query where trust is the ranking factor.
- **Mechanically clean.** Zero banned words, zero em/en dashes, zero British spellings, zero paragraphs over 60 words, correct H1 to H3 nesting throughout.

**MISSING** (critical absent content — fill second):
1. The promised quick-reference table does not exist — "Which business plan option should you choose?" — The draft says "Use the table below as a quick reference:" and no table follows. Publish blocker.
2. What's included at each tier — throughout the four route sections — The brief's #1 target gap. The draft even diagnoses it ("the title 'business plan writer' alone does not tell you exactly what you are paying for") then never fills it.
3. How to spot an overcharge — no section exists — The brief's second unique edge. No red flags, no scope-creep warning, no revision-fee traps.
4. Six of eight must-include keywords absent — document-wide — And they are this page's actual demand: "business plan writers cost" alone drew 338 GSC impressions at position 18.5.
5. Upmetrics' own price never stated — "Use business plan software" — The brief explicitly requires stating it plainly. It is $7-$49/user/month.
6. Cost by plan purpose, including E-2 visa — "Purpose of the plan" — One clause mentioning "visa-related plan" with no figure, against a 200/mo KD 1 keyword.
7. The free / $0 route is reduced to "free templates" — "Write it yourself" — SCORE and SBDCs provide free one-on-one plan mentoring, and WBC and VBOC are absent entirely.

**WEAK** (too shallow — deepen third):
1. "Factors that affect business plan costs" has zero figures across 301 words — a reader still cannot estimate their own quote.

3. Rush fee unquantified — the real premium is 25-50% above standard, or roughly $1,000 to compress to two weeks.
4. The hourly-vs-project FAQ gives no hourly rate — freelancers median ~$40/hr in a $25-$75 band, consultants $150-$500/hr.
5. The decision framework runs 128 words with no if-then criteria — reproducing the exact weakness the brief identifies in Butler Consultants.
6. Free-route treatment understates what SCORE/SBDC actually deliver (see MISSING 7).
