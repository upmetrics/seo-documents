## 2. Thesis & Key Messages

### Thesis Statement

> Ashcombe & Reyes Injury Law, PLLC seeks a $180,000 term loan from Heartland Trust Bank to launch a contingency-fee personal injury practice in Meadowbrook, Ohio — funded in large part by a $133,000 working-capital reserve built specifically to advance litigation costs while settlements are pending.

### Key Messages

1. A dedicated case-cost financing reserve — not a generic cash cushion — is what makes a contingency-fee practice bankable in Year 1, when net income is projected to run negative.
2. A blended average net fee of $9,500 per resolved case and a 45-day settlement collection cycle drive every other number in the model; the plan makes that chain visible instead of hiding it in vague language.
3. Growth from 48 to roughly 79 resolved cases over three years comes from adding one associate attorney and scaling marketing spend — not from an unexplained volume spike.
4. IOLTA trust-accounting compliance directly shapes how case costs are tracked, reconciled, and reported — it belongs in the financial narrative, not as a footnote.
5. The Year 1 net loss (-$77,822) is explained by one-time launch costs and loan interest during ramp-up, not by a flawed model — the firm turns profitable in Year 2 and cash-generative in Year 3.
