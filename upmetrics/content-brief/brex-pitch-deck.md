## 2. Why We'll Win

- **Every competing teardown is pre-acquisition.** Capital One completed its purchase of Brex on 7 April 2026 for $5.15B, down from a $12.3B peak valuation. Not one page in the top 10 mentions it. We can close the loop on a story everyone else left hanging: the deck's core argument was that legacy banks run on outdated technology and cannot serve startups, and eight years later a legacy bank bought the company for less than half its peak value.
- **We get the slide count and the round right, and nobody else does.** Alexander Jarvis and Slidebean both analyse 18 slides. The deck is 19. Sources also disagree on the round: Jarvis dates it 2019, Slidebean says 2018, and the widely repeated "$57M" figure is actually $7M Series A (April 2017) plus $50M Series B (June 2018) combined. We state it precisely and cite the primary source.
- **Real slide copy, not paraphrase.** We have the actual on-slide text for the load-bearing slides: the five beachhead criteria on slide 7 (underwriting arbitrage, big spenders, early adopters, high growth/LTV, concentrated), the four control features on slide 11, and the negative-churn spend ladder ($1.5k/month at pre-seed to $1.5M/month at Series D). Competitors describe these in general terms.
- **Four of the nine SERP results are near-empty.** PitchDeckHunt (180 words), Failory (120 words), BaseTemplates and SlideShare are galleries or lead magnets with no analysis. Half the first page is beatable on depth alone.
- **The series already ranks.** OpenAI sits at position 1.5 with 58% CTR, Stripe at 4.0, Anthropic at 2.8 within days of publishing. The template works, and Upmetrics DR 73 beats five of the seven ranked competitor domains.
