## 2. Why We'll Win

> The SERP is only half-contested: SBA, Salesforce, and Investopedia rank on domain authority with *generic* business-plan guides, while the two service-specific pages (LivePlan, Upwork) are shallow. A purpose-built, numbers-heavy guide wins on relevance + depth.

- **Only guide with worked service-business math.** LivePlan and Upwork both list "financial plan" as a section but show zero numbers. We include a real revenue model (billable hours × rate × utilization) and a break-even example — the #1 gap in the SERP.
- **A filled-in mini-example inline.** No competitor shows an actual worked example on the page. We include a short, concrete sample (e.g., a cleaning or consulting firm) so readers see what "done" looks like.
- **Real pricing guidance, not just "pick a model."** Competitors name hourly/retainer/project pricing and move on. We show how to choose and how capacity/utilization drives revenue — the hardest part for service founders.
- **Service-first framing throughout.** We lead with what's *different* about a service plan (no inventory, selling time/expertise, people-dependent) instead of burying it, as the generalist guides do.
- **6 unique FAQs + hub for Upmetrics' service-template library.** We answer tangential questions (length, solo operators, update cadence) and link out to the 30+ industry service templates Upmetrics already ranks for — an internal-link moat competitors can't match.
