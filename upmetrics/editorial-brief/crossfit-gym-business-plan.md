## 2. Thesis & Key Messages

### Thesis Statement

> CrossFit Ashford Ridge is asking for a $100,000 SBA 7(a) loan to open a coach-led CrossFit affiliate in a suburb with no affiliate within 11 miles. The box breaks even at about 83 active members, and growth comes from a 50-spot founding-member presale and member retention.

### Key Messages

1. **A CrossFit affiliate sells coaching, not floor space.** Every class has a CF-L1 or higher coach and a 16-athlete cap, so the business is limited by coaching hours more than by square footage. Big-box gyms and HIIT franchises can't copy that.
2. **The founder is the plan's main asset, and the plan shows it.** Nine years of coaching, five as head coach, with the Foundations and coach-development record to back the membership forecast.
3. **The numbers are deliberately conservative.** 80 average members in Year 1, 125 by Year 3, at $195 ARPM. A Year 1 loss of $26,851 is funded from a $33,400 working capital reserve, and debt service coverage reaches 1.85× in Year 2.
4. **Retention is the growth strategy.** At 3% monthly churn, an average member stays about 33 months and is worth ~$6,400. Keeping members is worth more than adding ad spend.
5. **The affiliation costs are real and fully budgeted.** $1,000 application fee, $4,500/year affiliate fee, CF-L1 certifications and liability insurance are all in the startup costs and the P&L.

### What to Borrow / What to Avoid

**Borrow:**
- Rolesville's schedule logic: each class time is tied to a named member type. Do the same for CrossFit Ashford Ridge's 39 weekly classes.
- Rolesville's new-athlete process (free intro → on-ramp → membership) and its pre-opening marketing timeline, but with dates and targets.
- The Playbook's metrics: churn, ARPM, member lifetime, square feet per athlete, break-even + 10% buffer. Use them by name in the plan.
- Two-Brain's chapter order and its "high-value coaching, not cheap access" argument.

**Avoid:**
- Placeholder pricing or "to be decided" language. Every price is fixed in Section 1B.
- Generic gym language (24/7 access, cardio machines, "members can work out at their own pace"). This is a coached class business.
- Unsourced industry figures. Cite the BLS, ACSM, CrossFit or the Upmetrics fitness statistics page.
- Sales-page tone. The plan reads like a lender submission.
