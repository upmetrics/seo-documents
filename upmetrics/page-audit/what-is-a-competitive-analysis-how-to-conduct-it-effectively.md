### Top 3 Problems

1. **Intent mismatch:** the page is framed as "...for a Business Plan" while all 8 organic top-10 results for "how to conduct a competitive analysis" are general, step-by-step guides. Result: avg position 43 for the primary keyword, and only 2 Ahrefs-tracked keywords.
2. **Stuck just outside page 1, with near-zero CTR:** 11,047 US impressions and 1 click in 6 months. Business-plan variants sit at position 15–17 (e.g., "business plan competitive analysis example": 1,740 impr, pos 15.1), and impressions fell ~70% after June (step-down on 2026-09-25).
3. **No supporting evidence or depth signals:** 0 external citations, no statistics, screenshots from 2021, and no answers to the 4 PAA questions (5 steps, 4 Ps, 5 C's, best tools). Competitors also cover topics the page skips: tools, Porter's Five Forces, ongoing monitoring, and a downloadable template inside the steps.

### Traffic Opportunity

> KD 36, 250 vol/mo, 3,600 traffic potential. Repositioning the page as a general "how to conduct" guide (keeping business-plan use as one application) could move it from 0 clicks to ~100–250 clicks/mo at top 5. Separately, pushing the business-plan cluster (~5,000 impr/6mo at pos 15–17) onto page 1 adds ~30–80 clicks/mo.
