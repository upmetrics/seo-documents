## WHAT'S WORKING

- **The foot-traffic correction is genuinely expert.** Telling the reader that a busy sidewalk means nothing if the passersby will not buy, and to visit during their own peak hours, is advice none of the six analyzed competitors gives.
- **The mistakes section is the strongest writing in the draft.** The six failure modes are behavioral rather than generic, and "choosing a second location too close to the first" is a cannibalization point no competitor covers.
- **The seven-step process is logically ordered** — requirements, then area, then budget, then site, then legal, then comparison, then final verification. A first-time founder can follow it.
- **Language mechanics are clean.** Zero banned words, zero em dashes, zero British spellings, short paragraphs, and only two sentences over 30 words in a 2,543-word draft.

## P0 BLOCKERS

**WRONG** (factual/internal contradictions — fix first):

1. **Leasing sold on "flexibility to move later"** — FAQ — The lease section two H2s earlier warns the reader that personal guarantees and assignment restrictions make locations "difficult to leave later." Make the flexibility claim conditional.
2. **Broker FAQ answers itself twice** — FAQ — Opens "Not really," then lists four things a broker does for you. Pick a position and lead with the condition.
3. **Verbatim duplicate paragraph** — Check zoning, licenses, and location restrictions — "Here, I'd suggest you do need to know whether anything in the lease…" repeats the paragraph above it almost word for word. Delete it.
4. **Mistakes section sits after the decision point** — Common business location mistakes to avoid — Warnings about liking the area too much and trusting broker claims arrive after the reader has been told they have a winner. Move it before "Compare your shortlisted locations."

**MISSING** (critical absent content — fill second):

1. **The "10 key factors" section was deleted** — whole article — This is the page's only click-earning asset (position 8 for "10 factors to consider when choosing a business location"). Restore a scannable factor list under its own H2.
2. **Seven of eight must-include keywords absent** — whole article — including "site selection" at 800/mo, the highest-volume term in the brief.
3. **Rent-to-revenue benchmarks withheld** — Set a realistic location budget — "Use a benchmark that fits your business" gives away a `UNIQUE EDGE` row. Publishable ranges exist: full-service restaurants 5–10% of gross sales, quick-service 6–12%, retail 5–10% base rent.
4. **No weighted scoring model** — Compare your shortlisted locations — The single biggest gap on this SERP per the brief, and the section argues against counting instead of delivering it.
5. **Zero free US data sources named** — whole article — Census Business Builder, County Business Patterns, BLS OEWS, and FBI Crime Data Explorer are all in the brief and all absent.
6. **Taxes and incentives dropped entirely** — the word "tax" does not appear in the draft, and the live page has H3s for both Taxes and Government incentives.
7. **Zero outbound links and one contextual internal link** — the brief supplies ten of each.
8. **Break-even by site, recovery from a wrong choice, and per-business-model strategy** — three `UNIQUE EDGE` rows with no coverage at all.
9. **"Types of business locations" was marked RETAIN and was dropped** — all six of the live page's FAQs were replaced with new ones.
10. **The image carries content that exists nowhere in the text** — "Start with the basics:" ends on a colon and the four items live only inside `image1.png`, which also has no alt text.

**WEAK** (too shallow — deepen third):

1. **Personal guarantee is a two-word bullet** — Check zoning, licenses, and location restrictions — the one lease term that can pierce an LLC gets no more weight than "renewal options."
2. **Comparison table's cost row runs backwards** — Compare your shortlisted locations — every other row grades Strong > Good > Average, but this one grades High cost as worst, so scanning gives the wrong answer.
3. **"How long does it take" FAQ declines to answer** — the brief flags this as a question nobody on the SERP answers, and the draft opens with "The time frame is not predetermined."
