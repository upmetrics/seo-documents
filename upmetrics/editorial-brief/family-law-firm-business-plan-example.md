## 2. Thesis & Key Messages

### Thesis Statement

> Stonebridge Legal, PLLC is a financially disciplined solo family law practice: it launches lean (one attorney, one paralegal), earns clients almost entirely through referrals and local search rather than paid marketing, and adds cost — staff, space, services — only after caseload, collections, and attorney capacity data justify it.

### Key Messages

1. A solo family law practice can be built on a realistic, bank-plausible financial model — not a padded "big firm" projection — and still break even within the first year.
2. Matter-type mix (flat-fee vs. hourly-plus-retainer) is the single biggest lever on both profitability and cash flow timing, and the plan should show the math, not just assert it.
3. Caseload capacity is a hard constraint for a solo attorney, not a soft planning assumption — the plan states the number and shows how it was reached.
4. Referrals and local search outperform paid acquisition for a defined local market; the marketing budget should reflect that (modest, not absent).
5. Staffing decisions (a second paralegal, then an associate attorney) should follow demonstrated utilization and collections data, not a growth target set in advance.
