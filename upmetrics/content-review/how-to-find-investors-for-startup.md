## WHAT'S WORKING

- **The keyword-to-audience mismatch is fixed.** The live page's H1 says "Small Business" while the URL says startup; this draft is written for startup founders raising equity throughout. That was the single highest-leverage change in the brief and the writer got it right.
- **The stage-to-funding-source table opens the article.** It answers "which of these can actually fund me" before listing channels, and it explicitly warns against jumping straight to VCs — the exact mistake this persona makes.
- **Platforms are named, not gestured at.** OpenVC, Signal by NFX, Angel Capital Association, Crunchbase, Dealroom, PitchBook, F6S, Y Combinator, Techstars, 500 Global, Wefunder, Republic, StartEngine. Every article competitor on this SERP stays vague; this is the intended edge and it lands.
- **The "look up who funded 5-10 similar startups" method** is the most useful thing in the draft — concrete, free, and it works for a founder with no network.
- **Facts on the named tools hold up.** M12's seed-to-Series-B AI/cloud/security focus, the Alexa Fund's current scope, Signal by NFX being free, and all three Reg CF platforms being active were independently verified and are accurate.
