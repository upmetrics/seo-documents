### Top 3 Problems

1. **The page is structurally missing the first and last steps of the process it claims to teach.** It opens at "Determine the cash flows from operating activities" and ends at "Prepare a cash flow statement" — there is no *opening/starting cash balance* step and no *ending balance / reconciliation* step. Every competitor that outranks it, and the AI Overview itself, uses the full opening-balance → operating → investing → financing → closing-balance sequence.
2. **Zero authoritative external citations and zero live backlinks.** The only 4 external links on the page are Upmetrics' own Facebook, X, LinkedIn and YouTube profiles. Ahrefs reports **0 live backlinks and 0 live referring domains** (4 referring domains existed historically and have all been lost).
3. **The FAQ answers the wrong query.** All 5 FAQ questions are about cash flow *forecasting* ("How often should I update my cash flow forecast?"), not about *creating a cash flow statement*. None of the 4 People Also Ask questions on this SERP are answered anywhere on the page, despite valid FAQPage schema already being in place.

## 4. SERP & INTENT ANALYSIS

### What Google is rewarding

- **Dominant intent:** Informational — instructional "how to build one" content.
- **Content types ranking:** 8 of the 9 organic results are educational guides on constructing a cash flow statement. Two pair the guide with an asset: WallStreetPrep (#4) bundles a **calculator**, Sage (#7) bundles a **downloadable Excel template**. One is a foreign government resource (business.gov.au, #6). No forum, Reddit, Quora, PDF or pure product page ranks.
- **What this means:** Google wants a complete, self-contained, step-by-step construction walkthrough — and rewards pages that also hand the reader an artifact they can use.

**Authority is not the gate.** Organic DR spans 36–92 with a median of 87, but **position 10 is american1cu.org at DR 36 with url_rating 0**, and position 2 is HBS with url_rating 0 and 1 referring domain — outranking Investopedia (DR 92, 23,596 referring domains, UR 42) at position 5. Combined with KD 12, this SERP is decided on page quality, not links.

### Intent match/mismatch

- **Current page type:** Blog article / guide.
- **Match status:** ⚠️ **Partial mismatch.**
- **Why:** The format is right but the framing is narrower than the query. The page scopes itself to "a cash flow statement **for a business plan**" — that phrase appears in the H2 spine ("Steps to create a cash flow statement for a business plan", "Example of a cash flow statement for a business plan"). Searchers for `how to create a cash flow statement` are not necessarily writing a business plan; the ranking pages treat the CFS as a standing financial-reporting task. The business-plan angle should become a *supporting* section, not the organizing frame. Compounding this, the walkthrough omits the opening-balance and ending-balance steps (§1, Problem 1), so it does not actually complete the task it promises.
