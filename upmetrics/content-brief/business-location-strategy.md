## Appendix A: Writing Tone — DO / DON'T

**DO:**
- Use real numbers everywhere — "$4,200/month" not "higher rent"
- Write to a founder with a shortlist, not a student learning a concept
- Say the uncomfortable thing: the cheapest location is usually the most expensive mistake
- Use second person ("your shortlist," "the site you're considering")
- Keep paragraphs to 2-4 sentences

**DON'T:**
- Don't describe location strategy as a discipline without showing the method
- Don't pad the factor list with items you can't make actionable
- Don't pitch Upmetrics outside the two designated sections
- Don't cite statistics from other verticals to fill a data gap (Berxi's physician stats are the cautionary example)
- Don't use any banned AI term (Appendix B)
