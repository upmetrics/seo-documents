## 2. Why We'll Win

> Every one of the 5 scraped competitors was flagged for the **same three gaps**. That's our opening.

- **We add a decision framework — the #1 gap.** WorkBoard, Bank of America, US Chamber, BDC, and KU MBA all *list* strategies but none tell the reader **which one to pick** for their stage, risk appetite, and resources. Our "how to choose" section (with a decision table) is the single biggest differentiator and a featured-snippet / AI-Overview play.
- **We make readers "run the numbers" — no competitor does.** Every competitor was dinged for zero financial modeling. We show how to forecast the revenue, cost, and cash-flow impact of a strategy *before* committing — Upmetrics' core strength.
- **Real numbers and named examples, at depth.** Competitors either cite no data (BofA, BDC) or drop shallow one-liners. We pair each strategy with a concrete example and a real stat (SBA, BLS, Gallup, PwC), going deeper than the 85–160-words-per-section competitors.
- **A "common growth mistakes" section — another universal gap.** No competitor covers pitfalls, failure modes, or when a strategy backfires. We do.
- **A unique FAQ (5–6 questions) sourced from PAA + competitor gaps.** Only WorkBoard has anything FAQ-like; ours answers the tangential questions (timeline, safest strategy, budget, measurement) competitors skip.
