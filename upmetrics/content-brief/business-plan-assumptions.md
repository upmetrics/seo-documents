## 2. Why We'll Win

- **We use real numbers; every competitor stays vague.** bizplanr, johnolivant, and planprojections all describe *what* to assume but rarely say *what value* to assume. We'll include a full worked example (a sample business with actual dollar figures, growth %, margins, and lead times) that shows how each assumption flows into the financial statements.
- **We add scenario + sensitivity analysis — no competitor does.** Not one of the ranking pages explains best-case / base-case / worst-case modeling or how changing one assumption ripples through the forecast. This is our clearest differentiation and a strong linkable asset.
- **We embed an actual assumptions template/log, not just a "download" CTA.** johnolivant and planprojections both *link* to a checklist but don't show one. We'll put a fill-in-the-blank assumptions log right in the article.
- **We cover "fatal" / red-flag assumptions** — the assumptions that sink plans (assuming demand exists, assuming financing is guaranteed, assuming costs stay fixed, assuming key staff stay). Only the Purdue PDF touches this, and it's a hard-to-read academic document.
- **We bring the investor/lender lens.** We'll explain how a loan officer or investor stress-tests your assumptions — a natural fit for Upmetrics' audience and a topic every competitor ignores.

- [Financial Modeling Software](https://upmetrics.co/features/financial-modeling) — Place in: H2: Build assumption-driven projections with Upmetrics
