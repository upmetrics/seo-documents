#### Performance context

The standard CTR-vs-position check does not apply here. Every meaningful query sits at position 39-92, far outside the benchmark table, where expected CTR is effectively zero regardless of copy quality.

| business startup checklist template | 204 | 66.6 | 0% | n/a | Ranking problem |
| business startup checklist excel | 167 | **39.3** | 0% | n/a | Best position — template/spreadsheet intent |

**Classification: Minor polish.** The current meta is structurally sound (55 and 149 characters, keyword front-loaded). Two real issues justify a change:

1. **Meta title is identical to the H1.** Both read "Business Startup Checklist: 18 Steps, Costs & Timelines". The meta title should take a different angle.
2. **The title and description promise something the content does not deliver.** Both advertise costs and timelines ("Costs & Timelines", "the real cost, time, and prerequisite for all 18 steps"). The rewritten content deliberately does *not* give per-step costs or timelines — it repeatedly says fees and processing times vary by state and tells the reader to check locally. A searcher who clicks for costs and finds none bounces, which is the opposite of what this page needs while it is trying to climb.

The suggested rewrite pivots to what the page genuinely offers and what the data shows people want: **template / printable / spreadsheet intent** (371 combined impressions across the "template" and "excel" queries, and the best position on the page at 39.3).
