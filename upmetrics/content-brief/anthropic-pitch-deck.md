## Appendix A: Writer Guardrails

**Fact-checking rules for this article (non-negotiable):**
1. Do not write "this deck raised $580M from FTX." The Series B closed in April 2022; the hosted deck is dated February 2023. Say the deck comes from the period between the Series B and the Series C, and that several posts attribute it to the Series B.
