### UGC Insights — Reddit & Quora

**No Reddit or Quora pages appear in the top 10 SERP — the spec's UGC scrape does not apply.** A supplementary web search was run instead to capture founder sentiment. Treat the following as **aggregated secondary reporting, not verbatim quotes from a thread** — the writer should not attribute these to a named person or a specific post.

**Recurring positive sentiment:**
- Works well as a starting structure for a first-time founder with no pitching experience
- Simple, replicable, and useful as a checklist of gaps to fill

**Recurring criticism:**
- Founders treat it as a finished blueprint rather than a starting point
- It hasn't kept pace with how investors evaluate decks today
- Several argue the Team slide belongs near the front, because a strong team lends credibility to everything after it

**How this shaped the outline:**
- H2 *What the Sequoia template gets wrong in 2026* exists because of the "hasn't kept pace" criticism
- H3 *When to change the order* exists because of the Team-slide-first argument
- FAQ *Is the Sequoia template right for a pre-seed startup with no traction?* comes from the "starting point, not blueprint" theme
