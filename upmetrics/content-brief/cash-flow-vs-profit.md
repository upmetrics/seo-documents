## 2. Why We'll Win

- **We'll be the only result with a complete, realistic small-business worked example.** HBS uses Apple-scale financial statements (numbers in the millions); AFP, GoCardless and Hartford describe the profitable-but-cash-broke scenario qualitatively with no numbers. Reddit's top-voted threads are literally people begging for "an example" — we give them one with real dollar figures they can follow.
