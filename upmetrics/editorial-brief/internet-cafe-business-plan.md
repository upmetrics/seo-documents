## 2. Thesis & Key Messages

### Thesis Statement

> Lantern Point Internet Café & Print seeks a $60,000 SBA 7(a) loan to open a 10-workstation document and computer-access storefront in Alief, southwest Houston. The plan shows that a neighborhood internet café stays profitable because printing, document prep and digital help, not computer time, cover the rent.

### Key Messages

1. **Computer access brings people in; document services pay the bills.** Computer time is 45% of transactions but only 22.5% of revenue. Document prep alone is 36%.
2. **Libraries are the real competitor, and the plan has to say so.** Lantern Point wins on evening and Saturday hours, accepting cash, having no time limits, and offering hands-on help. It does not try to beat free.
3. **Location decides the business.** It needs a bus line, walk-in visibility and dense apartment housing within 3 miles, close to the people who need forms, photos and résumés.
4. **Workstation security is a selling point.** Every session wipes on logout, scans are deleted after sending, and customers can see that their documents don't stay on the computer.
5. **The financials are conservative.** Prices stay flat, volume grows 10% a year, Year 1 shows a small loss from startup costs, and debt service is covered with a thin cushion and a $24,100 cash reserve.

## Why This Matters

- Most internet cafe sample plans online describe gaming lounges or coffee shops with Wi-Fi. A founder planning a print-and-documents storefront has almost nothing realistic to model from.
- The plan answers a lender's first question ("why would anyone pay when the library is free?") with specific evidence: [Houston Public Library resumed charging for printing in November 2024](https://houstonlibrary.org/blog/houston-public-library-resumes-print-charges) and accepts card payment only.
- Smartphone dependence is highest among lower-income and Hispanic adults ([Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/chart/young-adults-hispanic-adults-those-with-lower-incomes-are-more-likely-to-rely-on-smartphones-for-internet/)). Those are the people who still need a place to print, scan and type documents.
- An $85K, bank-ready plan with fully reconciled statements fits what first-time founders searching this term can actually raise, and it shows Upmetrics' financial forecasting in action.

### What to Borrow / What to Avoid

**Borrow:**
- Reference 1's location rationale (foot traffic, parking, $/sq ft) and its candid risk section → our "Location & Lease Analysis" and "Risks & Mitigation" chapters.
- Reference 2's security controls (camera retention, network encryption, logs), extended into session wiping and document deletion → our "Workstation Security" chapter.
- Reference 2's naming of the library as a competitor, but treat it as the main competitor, not a negligible one.
- Reference 3's honesty about weak demand for internet access alone. That honesty is the setup for our thesis.

**Avoid:**
- All three references publish numbers that don't reconcile. Every number in our plan must come from Section 6B.
- Gaming nights, coffee/espresso revenue, or "upscale" positioning. Lantern Point is an affordable neighborhood service counter.
- Unsourced market sizes. Use the cited sources in Section 6, and describe estimates as estimates.
- First-person "we" voice (Reference 2) and promotional adjectives (Reference 1).
