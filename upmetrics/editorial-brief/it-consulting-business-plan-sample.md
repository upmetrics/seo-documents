## 2. Thesis & Key Messages

### Thesis Statement

> A credible IT consulting plan is not a services list — it is a model of how billable time, recurring revenue, and client selection combine into a profitable, defensible firm; this example shows the numbers and the operating decisions behind them.

### Key Messages

1. An IT consulting firm lives or dies on **billable utilization** — the percentage of paid staff time that is actually invoiced. The plan makes this the central operating metric, not an afterthought.
2. **Recurring revenue** (vCIO/advisory retainers) is what turns a feast-or-famine consultancy into a fundable business. The example deliberately splits revenue ~65% project / ~35% recurring and explains why.
3. **Client and project selection** is a strategy, not a reaction. The plan defines who Clearpath serves, who it turns away, and how it qualifies projects to protect margin and utilization.
4. **Service standardization** (productized packages) is how a small firm scales delivery without hiring senior people for every engagement.
5. The financials are internally consistent and conservative — a lender or SBA reviewer could check the math and find it holds.
