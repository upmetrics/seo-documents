**Traffic Opportunity**

> KD 14, 200 searches/mo for the primary keyword alone (plus meaningful adjacent-query volume: "projected cash flow statement" alone drew 229 impressions in 6 months). This page currently converts 100% of its ~950 six-month impressions across 168 queries into **zero clicks**. Resolving the cannibalization, closing the content depth gap vs. competitors, and refreshing for current search intent could realistically move this cluster from 0 clicks/month to 50-150 clicks/month at top-10, or 200+ at top-3 — without needing to out-authority the DR 80-99 competitors, since KD is low.
