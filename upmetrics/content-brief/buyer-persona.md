## 2. Why We'll Win

- **The only "no customer data" path in the SERP.** Salesforce, HubSpot and Semrush all start from CRM data, analytics or an existing customer base. buyerpersona.com requires 10+ recorded buyer interviews. We give a small business owner a free, zero-data route: competitor reviews, forums, search data, Census/BLS data, and 5 quick validation conversations.
- **Small business examples, not enterprise ones.** Competitor examples are "Enterprise Emma" (VP Ops, 500+ staff), "HR Harry", "Accounting Alice" — all mid-to-large B2B. We show a local B2C example, a B2B small-business example, a service-business example and a negative persona.
- **Personas applied to the business plan.** No competitor connects personas to customer analysis, market sizing, pricing or product decisions. This is Upmetrics' home turf and our clearest differentiator.
- **A usable one-page template inside the article**, not gated behind a form (Salesforce and HubSpot gate theirs; Semrush only lists categories).
- **Sourced stats only.** HubSpot cites 71%/96%/94%/2x figures with no named sources; Salesforce cites one vendor stat. We cite named, dated research (McKinsey, Salesforce State of Sales, Census, BLS).
