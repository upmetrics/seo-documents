**The contractors (mention only in Operations):** one outside recipe tester, a home cook in Katy, Texas, who cooks each new recipe once in her own kitchen and reports back on timing, steps, and taste ($30 a recipe plus Supperwell reimburses ingredients inside the $9.50-a-batch budget); and one freelance proofreader who checks each post and its recipe card within 48 hours ($20 a post). **Content facts (use in Recipes, Meal Plans & Revenue Streams):**
- Every recipe serves four, is tested at least three times in Tessa's kitchen plus once by the outside tester, and lists cost per serving (2027 target: under $3.50 a serving on most recipes)

- Publishing rhythm: about 2–3 new recipes a week (10 a month in 2027, 12 from 2028), plus 4 / 6 / 8 refreshed older recipes a month

## 2. Thesis & Key Messages

### Thesis Statement

> A recipe blog earns slowly: each tested recipe costs money up front and pays it back over months or years, one pageview at a time. Supperwell Kitchen shows how a two-year-old food blog uses a small SBA microloan to publish more tested recipes, plans for a first-year loss and late ad payments, and grows steady income from ads, affiliates, and sponsorships while repaying the loan from a cash reserve and then from earnings.

### Key Messages

1. **Revenue comes late, and the plan says so.** A new recipe reaches only about half its normal traffic by month six, and Raptive pays about 50 days after the month ends. 2027 shows a $6,687 loss, Tessa takes no pay, and the $8,800 reserve keeps month-end cash above $7,098.
2. **One recipe costs $88 in cash and about $288 with Tessa's time.** The average recipe earns about $7.61 a month once it matures, repays its cash cost in about 18 months and its full cost in about 4 years. Half of all recipes rarely rank; one in twenty becomes a hit that repays its full cost within a year.
3. **Sponsorships make the gap bearable.** One $1,300 sponsorship covers the cash cost of about 15 new recipes. Sponsorships grow from 17% of revenue in 2027 to 34% in 2029, while ads stay the base.
4. **Readers who come back matter more than search.** Search referrals to small publishers fell sharply in 2025–2026, so Supperwell builds its email list (4,500 → 24,000) and Pinterest reach, and refreshes older recipes every month.
5. **Small loan, clear repayment.** $283.59 a month. Debt coverage is 1.59× in 2028 and 2.96× in 2029; in 2027 the reserve pays the loan. A 30% traffic drop for six months still leaves cash above $8,900 when Tessa slows new recipes and pauses paid promotion.
