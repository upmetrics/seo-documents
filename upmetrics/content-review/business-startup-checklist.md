## WHAT'S WORKING

- **The step sequence is right.** Validate → cost it → register → tax/licence → money → operations → customers → launch test is the order a founder actually needs, and it beats the flat lists every competitor ships.
- **The worked cash example is the sharpest thing in the draft.** Reframing "startup cost" as "cash needed to survive to revenue" ($12,000 to launch + $8,000 runway = $20,000 real need) is the insight that separates useful from generic. Arithmetic verified on both examples.
- **The EIN guidance is correct and protective.** Sequencing state formation before the EIN, and explicitly warning readers not to pay a third party for a free IRS application, is right and most competitors get it wrong or skip it.
- **Language quality is high.** Zero banned words, zero em dashes, zero British spellings, zero paragraphs over 60 words, only three sentences over 30 words. This is a clean draft mechanically.
- **"Monthly SEO audits for dental practices"** is a real, specific example that could not be copy-pasted from a competitor.
