## 2. Why We'll Win

- **Nobody on this SERP organizes letters by recipient.** All five scraped competitors teach the same generic five steps and then hand over one generic letter. But a bank underwriter, an SBA lender, an angel investor, a VC associate, a commercial landlord, and a grant committee each want different things in paragraph one. The reader already knows which one they're writing to — no page currently lets them jump straight to it.
- **Nobody makes the letters copy-pasteable.** Proposify's three templates are **screenshots**. Examples.com's are **downloadable ZIPs** pointing at third-party sites. Loopio gates its five behind a form. Indeed has exactly one inline text sample. We publish six as selectable plain text in the page body. This is the single highest-value fix on the SERP and it costs nothing.
- **Zero competitors have a FAQ — and the SERP has a question block at position 4.** Four PAA questions are sitting unclaimed. Our seven-question FAQ targets them plus the "cover sheet" and "executive summary" confusions that drive real search volume.
- **Nobody explains the hard part: how to state the money.** Every competitor says "mention your financial projections." None shows how to compress an ask, a use of funds, and a repayment or return story into one paragraph. Both Indeed and Proposify were independently flagged for this exact gap in scraping.
- **The image pack at position 3 is unowned by any organic result.** Six image slots currently go to Proposify, template.net, examples.com, and resumaker.ai. Rendered previews of our six letters are a direct shot at that pack.

**Upmetrics assets to embed:**
- [Business Plan Examples](https://upmetrics.co/features/business-plan-examples) — Place in: H2 "Business plan cover letter templates by recipient" (intro paragraph, as the thing the letter is attached to)
- [Create a Business Plan](https://upmetrics.co/create-business-plan) — Place in: closing section "Send it with a plan that backs it up"

**Original value-adds competitors can't match:**
- Six complete letters as selectable body text, each tagged with the recipient and the one thing that reader cares about most
- An annotated weak-vs-strong rewrite of the same letter — no competitor shows before/after
- A one-paragraph financial-ask formula with a filled-in example using real dollar figures
- A delivery section covering email body vs. attached PDF vs. printed copy, including what to put in the email subject line
