## 2. Thesis & Key Messages

### Thesis Statement

> Blue Avenue Records seeks a $75,000 SBA-backed loan to launch a lean, digital-first Americana label in Rivermont, NC — signing 2–3 emerging artists on profit-split and license deals, recouping controlled release budgets, and building a profitable, master-owning catalog that compounds in value year over year.

### Key Messages

1. A small indie label can be fundable and profitable without chasing a viral hit — the model is controlled release budgets, disciplined recoupment, and a catalog that earns for years.
2. Master ownership is the asset. The plan shows how retaining masters (via profit-split and license deals) turns each release into a long-term, sellable catalog line rather than a sunk marketing cost.
3. Recoupment is the engine that protects the label's cash. Every recording and marketing dollar is recovered from release revenue before artist profit splits begin — the plan models this explicitly.
4. Performance-based marketing keeps risk low: ad spend scales to releases that show real traction, not to every release equally.
5. The financials are realistic and lender-ready — a thin Year 1 profit growing to solid Year 3 margins, with cash reserves that never run dry and a balance sheet that balances every year.
